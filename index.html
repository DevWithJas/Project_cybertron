🧠 About Me

I'm Jas — a final-year B.Tech student in Automation & Robotics and the founder of a safety-focused tech initiative. My work lies in designing and building intelligent systems that understand risk, reduce uncertainty, and make urban spaces safer for all of us.

I care about execution — about turning abstract ideas into tools that actually work, under pressure, and at scale.

For me, safety isn't just a domain — it's a responsibility.

My journey began with a fascination for how technology can solve real-world problems. Over the years, I've worked on projects ranging from real-time traffic analysis to predictive crime mapping, always with a focus on practical impact. I'm driven by the challenge of balancing technical complexity with user-centric design.
